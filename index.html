About Me

A career built on creative range & versatility

Born and raised in Southern New Jersey, I attended Temple University in Philadelphia and studied Film & Media Arts before packing my bags for Los Angeles — and the rest is a career that’s genuinely hard to summarize in a single line.

I’ve managed productions on TV sets, wrangled creative timelines at entertainment agencies, coordinated Broadway marketing campaigns, and most recently oversaw medical/legal/regulatory (MLR) routing for pharmaceutical brands at a healthcare agency. The through-line is the same everywhere: I make sure the work gets done — on time, on budget, and with the team still intact.

I’m a strong communicator and natural organizer who genuinely loves the creative side of project management — getting close enough to the work to care about it, and disciplined enough to always deliver it.
